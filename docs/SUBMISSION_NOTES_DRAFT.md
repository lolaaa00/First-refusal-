# Submission notes draft

FirstRefusal is a contract-only GenLayer primitive for rights of first refusal. A grantor and holder freeze an exact right definition, public-offer scope, required terms, match window and expiry. When the grantor receives a third-party offer, validators independently fetch the frozen public evidence and agree only on two bounded questions: whether the source supports the exact declared offer and whether that offer falls inside the frozen ROFR scope. All consequences are deterministic. Covered offers block the third party while the holder's match window is open. The holder can exercise only by reproducing the exact canonical terms hash. Waiver/expiry releases only the original third party under those exact terms. Ambiguous, unavailable, contradicted or unsafe evidence fails closed. A separate ProtectedTransfer IC pins the right hash and provides the cross-contract enforcement boundary with one-use offer consumption. No frontend; Studionet 61999 only.
