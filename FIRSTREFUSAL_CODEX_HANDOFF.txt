NON-NEGOTIABLE PROTOCOL INVARIANTS
----------------------------------
Preserve these even if implementation details must change:

1. A right is not binding until the holder ratifies it.
2. Right definition is immutable and hash-pinned.
3. Only the grantor may register a third-party offer.
4. Offer terms are canonicalized deterministically and hashed.
5. Required term keys are deterministic.
6. One unresolved/open offer encumbers a right at a time.
7. Public evidence must be HTTPS and validators independently re-fetch it.
8. The semantic surface is bounded:
       evidence: SUPPORTS / CONTRADICTS / INSUFFICIENT / UNAVAILABLE / UNSAFE
       coverage: COVERED / OUTSIDE_SCOPE / AMBIGUOUS
9. Coverage is consequential only when evidence SUPPORTS the exact declared offer.
10. AMBIGUOUS / UNAVAILABLE / CONTRADICTED / QUARANTINED fail closed.
11. A COVERED offer blocks third-party transfer during the match window.
12. Holder exercise must reproduce the EXACT frozen terms hash. No LLM
    substantial-equivalence shortcut is allowed.
13. EXERCISED authorizes only the holder + exact terms hash.
14. WAIVED / RELEASED / OUTSIDE_SCOPE authorize only the original third party
    + exact terms hash.
15. Wrong right hash, wrong buyer or wrong terms hash must always deny.
16. The model never invents a price, chooses a buyer, rewrites terms, grants the
    right, waives the right, or controls the match window.
17. The primitive never claims to enforce transfers in systems that do not
    integrate it.
